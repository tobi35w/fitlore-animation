Title: Missed One Workout? Don’t Throw Away the Month

Keywords: missed workout; fitness habits; consistency; habit formation; perfectionism; workout streaks; FitLore.

Marcus misses a workout and almost bins his routine. An unfinished brick wall helps him see why one blank square does not erase everything he has done.

This is an original illustrative FitLore story, not a documented fitness transformation. In Lally and colleagues’ study of everyday habits, missing one opportunity did not materially affect habit formation. That finding does not mean repeated omissions are irrelevant or prescribe daily workouts.

Source: Lally et al., How are habits formed: Modelling habit formation in the real world, European Journal of Social Psychology (2010). https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674
